FAQ

What to know before you start saving with LinQ.

What can I save to LinQ?

Save links to articles, posts, videos, and other web pages from apps that share a link through the iPhone share sheet.

How do I save a link?

Open the page or post, tap Share, and choose LinQ. You can pick a collection before you save.

What’s the difference between a Collection and a Space?

Collections organize your own bookmarks. A Shared Space lets you collect links with other people and choose who can view or edit them.

How do I find a bookmark again?

Open its collection, look under Starred if you marked it as a favorite, or search using something you remember about the link.

What if LinQ doesn’t appear in the share sheet?

Swipe to the end of the app row, tap More, and look for LinQ. You can add it to Favorites to keep it near the front.

Can I add a note to a bookmark?

Yes. Add a note as you save a link to remember why you kept it or what to look at later.

Can I change a bookmark’s cover?

Open the bookmark editor to replace the preview image with one of your own, or remove the cover.

How do I invite someone to a Space?

Open a Space and use its invite options. You can give someone permission to add links or share it with view-only access.
